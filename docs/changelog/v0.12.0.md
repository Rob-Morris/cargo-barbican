# v0.12.0 — 2026-06-22

**Summary** Render reviewed advisory exceptions in pin-check

## Features

- `pin-check` now renders accepted reviewed advisory exceptions in the
  `Allowed policy exceptions:` register, e.g. `serde@1.0.228 RUSTSEC-2026-0001
  accepted by reviewed family serde-family (<review-record>), review by <date>`.
- An advisory exception renders as accepted only when its binding holds: the
  family review record exists AND the exception's crate resolves to the exact
  reviewed version and checksum. Drifted, checksum-mismatched, or
  missing-review-record exceptions are suppressed from the register while
  `pin-check` still fails on the underlying drift.

## Safety

- The advisory-exception register routes through the shared terminal
  control-character escaper at the stdout boundary, matching the other CLI
  render sinks.
- The report exposes the binding decision through the library
  (`advisory_exceptions_with_matching_resolved_target`), which enforces the
  resolved-target match; the binary layers review-record success on top. The
  raw `advisory_exceptions` accessors document that they apply no binding and
  that callers suppressing findings must enforce resolved-target match,
  review-record success, and `review_by` expiry.

## Notes

- `review_by` expiry is not yet enforced; pin-check renders exceptions for
  display only. Advisory suppression and `review_by` expiry enforcement land
  with `audit` reconciliation in a later cluster.
