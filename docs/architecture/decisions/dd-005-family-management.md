# DD-005: Reviewed Family Discovery and Explicit Extension

## Context

Maintainers need to discover the exact review scope before extending an existing
family, including families sharing evidence and policies generated by pin add.

## Decision

Family management remains under the existing `pin` namespace: `pin list`,
`pin show <family>` and `pin add --family <existing-name> <crate>...`.
Read-only discovery/detail and explicit one-family batches form one useful
workflow. A family subgroup adds nesting without an independent domain owner.

Legacy single-target creation and registry refresh keep their meanings.
Addition has its own pure plan and independently expected typed policy: it
does not invent an old identity or inherit refresh's grant withdrawal. The
binary shares captured inputs, confined records and PENDING-first publication
because these are the same protection rules for two real callers.

Shared configured records remain one review boundary. Addition appends once
and reopens every citing family visibly; it does not retarget or split records.
Exact selected-family matches are idempotent without changing facts, evidence
or grants. Conflicting/foreign targets reject the batch, including mixed batches.

## Consequences

Source-preserving insertion supports actual generated registry/Git member
subtables and verifies typed equality with the independent plan. Unsupported
layouts refuse before evidence writes. A retained PENDING append is safer than
deleting shared history when later publication fails.

Family views render declared facts and available parity, including unavailable
optional evidence, without adding a policy engine or new PASS authority.
Review completion and independent enforcement retain their existing owners.

## Alternatives Considered

A separate family subgroup would split the existing pin vocabulary. Reusing
refresh plans would conflate additions with replacement and grant withdrawal.
Whole-file serialisation would obscure reviewable diffs; unsupported layouts
therefore retain a manual non-applying path.
